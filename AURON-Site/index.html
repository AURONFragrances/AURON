OUR FOUNDATION

The Anniversary Story

How an intimate gift grew into an obsessive commitment to fragrance quality.

01 / THE REQUEST

Something Truly Personal

AURON began quietly as a gift. As my wife and I approached our 10th anniversary, she asked for something deeply personal—not simply a commercial luxury item I thought she might like, but something that truly reflected who she was.

I decided to learn the craft of perfumery with a specific challenge in mind: formulating a sophisticated fragrance inspired by her favorite indulgence—chocolate-covered strawberries.

FORMULATION JOURNAL & RAW ABSOLUTES

02 / THE PASSION

From Gift to Perfume House

What started as an anniversary project rapidly grew into a devotion. As I studied raw perfume materials—Citrus extracts, aldehydes and lactones, making natural botanicals, and clean amber molecules—I became captivated by the chemistry and emotion of scent creation.

That single anniversary gift became the foundational benchmark for AURON: fragrances formulated with absolute devotion to quality, unconstrained by artificial retail price tiers.

Why We Reject Luxury Clichés

No Corporate Disruption Jargon

We do not claim to "disrupt" or "redefine" luxury. We simply focus on meticulous ingredient selection, precise aging/maceration, and beautiful physical presentation.

No Artificial Exclusivity

We don't create fake waiting lists or inflate prices to signal prestige. We price our fragrances fairly based on genuine formulation costs.
